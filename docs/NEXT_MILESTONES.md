# Next implementation milestones

1. Confirm deployment geography and launch languages with local partner input.
2. Complete and locally verify milestone 1 authentication/RBAC/service-area access.
3. Patient profile and consent lifecycle with minimum necessary data.
4. Community health worker workflows and supervisor administration.
5. Administrative appointment-request coordination (not clinical triage).
6. Verified facility/clinician directory and provider-controlled availability.
7. Pharmacy directory and availability requests (no prescribing).
8. Offline-first synchronization with encryption, conflict handling and auditability.
9. Language packs and voice UX, validated by native speakers; translation must not alter clinical meaning.
10. Security assessment, privacy/legal review, clinical governance and controlled pilot readiness.

Every milestone needs automated tests, threat review, accessibility review and documented acceptance criteria. External credentials (SMS, hosting, monitoring), legal review, clinical approvals and local partner decisions are not included in this repository.
