# Rollout plan for a large-area programme

Do not launch across a large geography on day one. Build for scale, then expand through controlled waves.

## Wave 0 — discovery and governance
Map districts, blocks, villages, connectivity, languages, facilities, workforce and emergency pathways.
Secure implementation partners. Select the first clinical use cases with local clinicians.

## Wave 1 — controlled pilot
Choose a small, representative cluster. Test registration, assisted access, verified provider directory,
appointment coordination and follow-up. Keep AI limited to non-clinical language support and clinician-
reviewed intake summaries.

## Wave 2 — block expansion
Add facilities and trained health workers in batches. Monitor failed syncs, appointment completion,
referral closure, language comprehension, support tickets and safety incidents.

## Wave 3 — district and multi-district scale
Introduce area-level operations teams, service-level objectives, capacity planning, disaster recovery,
formal vendor management and independent audits.

## Suggested operational metrics
- Successful onboarding and consent completion
- Appointment request-to-confirmation time
- Consultation completion and referral closure rates
- Failed sync rate and app crash-free sessions
- Directory freshness and provider credential validity
- User comprehension and language-related support requests
- Safety incidents, near misses and complaint resolution time

Never use raw patient-level health data for public dashboards.
