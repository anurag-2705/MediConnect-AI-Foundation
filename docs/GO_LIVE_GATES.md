# Go-live gates — all required before real patients

## Governance
- [ ] Named operating organisation and accountable clinical lead
- [ ] Written partnerships/permissions with participating facilities and local health authorities as applicable
- [ ] Clinician-approved symptom intake, escalation and referral protocols
- [ ] Verified registered practitioners and documented credential rechecks
- [ ] Verified emergency contacts and facility directory for every launch area

## Legal and privacy
- [ ] India-specific legal review of telemedicine, DPDP and applicable health rules
- [ ] Data inventory, purpose limitation, notices, consent and withdrawal process
- [ ] Retention/deletion schedule and grievance/contact mechanism
- [ ] Child and guardian consent workflow reviewed by counsel
- [ ] Contracts and data-processing terms with all vendors

## Security and reliability
- [ ] Threat model and privacy impact assessment
- [ ] Independent penetration test and remediation
- [ ] Encryption, key management, backups and restore drill
- [ ] Access-control tests across every role and service area
- [ ] Incident response and breach communication plan
- [ ] Load, outage and poor-network tests

## Field readiness
- [ ] Local-language translations reviewed by native speakers
- [ ] Usability tests with patients and community health workers
- [ ] Low-connectivity and shared-device tests
- [ ] Support and escalation team trained
- [ ] Pilot success metrics and stop criteria approved
- [ ] Informed pilot participation and feedback process

Do not launch patient-facing clinical functions while any critical gate remains open.
