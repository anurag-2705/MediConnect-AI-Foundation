# Open decisions before the first real pilot

1. Which state(s), districts and blocks are in the first deployment geography?
2. Which launch languages are required? Suggested starting set for a Bihar pilot: Hindi, Bhojpuri,
   Maithili and English, subject to local validation.
3. Which organisation will operate the platform and be accountable for patient data?
4. Which facilities, clinicians, pharmacies and community health worker groups have agreed to participate?
5. What verified emergency numbers and referral pathways apply in each service area?
6. Is the first release limited to appointment/referral coordination, or will a clinician-led telemedicine
   workflow be included from day one?
7. Which SMS, video and hosting vendors are approved, and what are their data-processing terms?

No answer should be guessed. Record decisions and approvals before enabling the corresponding feature.
