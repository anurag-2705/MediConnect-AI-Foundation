# Safety and clinical governance

## Non-negotiable boundary
MediConnect AI is a care-access and coordination tool. It is not an autonomous doctor.
No model output may independently diagnose, prescribe, recommend a drug or dose, or tell a person
to delay urgent care.

## Symptom intake
- Ask only clinician-approved, necessary questions.
- Show a clear notice that the intake is not a diagnosis.
- Escalation rules must be authored and signed off by qualified clinicians.
- If an emergency may be present, the product must direct the person to immediate local emergency
  assistance and display only verified local contact instructions.
- Do not invent ambulance availability or imply that an emergency dispatch integration exists.
- Every AI-generated summary shown to a clinician must be labelled AI-generated and editable.

## Teleconsultation
Before enabling consultations, implement provider identity verification, patient identity checks,
informed consent, consultation documentation, continuity-of-care workflow, privacy/security controls,
and clinician judgment on whether an in-person examination is needed. Review current Indian
Telemedicine Practice Guidelines and obtain qualified legal/clinical advice.

## Provider and pharmacy verification
Verify professional registration and facility credentials using authoritative processes. Keep
verification status, reviewer, evidence reference, timestamp and expiry/recheck date.
Pharmacy stock must show last-updated time and source. Never imply stock is guaranteed.

## Human factors
Test with low-literacy users, local-language speakers, older adults, people with disabilities and
shared-phone households. Provide assisted use and non-voice alternatives. Never make voice the only path.
